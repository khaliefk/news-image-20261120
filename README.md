# Friday 20 November 2026 — the world in one image

A newspaper-style front page summing up Friday 20 November 2026 in six
stories, drawn as illustrated panels at 1024x1024. `main.ts` (Node 22,
standard library only) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`; it uses
no network and no randomness, so two runs write identical bytes. The image
was generated by the agent with an image model from the day's verified
reporting; the prompt and both attempts are in `generate.md`.

Layout: a full-width lead story (the Shenzhen peace declaration) above
five stories in a 3 + 2 grid, each with its own illustration and
headline. All six were verified against the sources below. The build ran
in the small hours of Friday 20 November WIB, so the front page covers
the news cycle as it stood then: events of 19 November first reported
in the 20 November cycle sit next to the landfall and sales figures
dated 20 November itself. Each story below gives its real date.

## The six stories

1. **Peace declaration in Shenzhen** — US President Donald Trump,
   China's President Xi Jinping and Russia's President Vladimir Putin
   issue the "China-Russia-U.S. Heads of State Declaration toward
   Peace" after their first-ever trilateral meeting at Kylin Villa,
   Shenzhen, on Thursday 19 November, on the sidelines of the APEC
   summit. The text says "a nuclear war cannot be won and must never
   be fought" and promises a dialogue on reducing nuclear risks, plus
   UN reform and higher refined-oil output. It sets no timetable and
   says nothing about Ukraine or Iran. The joint text was published
   and reported in the 20 November cycle.
   Sources: Reuters wrapup (published 20 Nov 2026); The Times live
   coverage (20 Nov 2026); China Ministry of Foreign Affairs text.
2. **Myanmar guilty of genocide** — The International Court of Justice
   in The Hague rules on Thursday 19 November that Myanmar committed
   genocide against the Rohingya in northern Rakhine state in 2017,
   the first time the court has held a state responsible for genocide
   in its 80-year history. The vote was nine to six. The court orders
   reparations and the safe, voluntary return of the displaced. The
   case was brought by The Gambia in 2019.
   Sources: UN News (19 Nov 2026); CNN (19 Nov 2026); Le Monde
   (20 Nov 2026).
3. **Cyclone Akvan hits India** — Severe Cyclonic Storm Akvan crosses
   India's east coast near Gopalpur, Odisha, around noon on Friday
   20 November, between Narsapur in Andhra Pradesh and Puri in
   Odisha, with sustained winds of 90-100 km/h gusting to 110 km/h.
   More than 300,000 people were moved out of vulnerable areas in
   Odisha, 423 rescue teams were deployed, and 69 trains were
   cancelled.
   Sources: India Meteorological Department bulletin (19 Nov 2026);
   The Daily Jagran live tracker (20 Nov 2026).
4. **Missile hits Riyadh tower** — A Houthi attack on Thursday
   19 November damages the PIF Tower, Riyadh's tallest skyscraper and
   headquarters of the Saudi sovereign wealth fund, in the King
   Abdullah Financial District. Windows were blown out across seven
   to eight floors and parts of the metal facade were bent. Saudi
   civil defence reported material damage and no injuries. The
   Houthis said they also targeted King Khalid International Airport.
   It was the second strike on the district in under two weeks, after
   the Aramco building on 8 November.
   Sources: Reuters (19 Nov 2026); Saudi Press Agency (19 Nov 2026).
5. **GTA VI: $2.7 billion** — Grand Theft Auto VI, out worldwide on
   Thursday 19 November for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, keeps
   selling through Friday 20 November. Market tracker Sensor Tower
   puts cumulative digital sales at an estimated 27.3 million copies
   and about $2.67 billion by 20 November, net of returns, with
   roughly 7.9 million copies added in the last 24 hours. Rockstar
   and Take-Two have published no official sales figure.
   Sources: Sensor Tower tracker via AppGazers (20 Nov 2026);
   Reuters via USA Today (19 Nov 2026).
6. **Anthropic closes at $97** — Anthropic's shares close at $97 on
   their first day of Nasdaq trading on Thursday 19 November, after
   pricing at $83 in a record $81 billion IPO that valued the Claude
   maker at about $1.5 trillion fully diluted. The shares touched
   $115 during the day, briefly valuing the company above $2
   trillion. The offering itself is due to close on Friday
   20 November.
   Sources: Reuters Trading Day (20 Nov 2026); CoinDesk
   (19 Nov 2026).

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`out/entry.png` is the 1024x1024 JPEG (about 285 KiB, under the 1 MiB cap).
